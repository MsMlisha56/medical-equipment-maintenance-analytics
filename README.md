Medical Equipment Maintenance Analytics

Project Overview

This is a Power BI portfolio project analyzing medical equipment maintenance data to understand maintenance costs, equipment downtime, repair time, work orders, and preventive versus corrective maintenance.

The project uses a synthetic medical equipment maintenance dataset created for analytical and portfolio purposes.

Objectives

- Analyze maintenance costs across equipment.
- Identify equipment with higher downtime and repair requirements.
- Compare preventive and corrective maintenance.
- Track maintenance work orders and repair time.
- Analyze maintenance cost trends over time.
- Identify areas that may benefit from improved preventive maintenance and maintenance planning.

Key Findings

- E002 (Dialysis Machine) shows the highest combination of maintenance cost, downtime, and repair requirements.
- Corrective maintenance accounts for KES 78,000 in maintenance costs and 71 hours of maintenance time.
- Preventive maintenance accounts for KES 48,000 in maintenance costs and 21 hours of maintenance time.
- The dataset contains 15 work orders, with total maintenance costs of KES 126,000, 92 downtime hours, and 53 repair days.

Recommendations

The analysis highlights opportunities to:

- Prioritize preventive maintenance for high-criticality equipment.
- Investigate equipment with consistently higher maintenance burdens.
- Monitor repeat failures and recurring faults.
- Improve maintenance resource and spare-parts planning.
- Regularly review maintenance cost, downtime, repair time, and work-order trends.

Note

This project uses a synthetic dataset and does not represent real hospital or employer data. It was developed as a portfolio project to demonstrate the application of data analytics and Power BI to medical equipment maintenance.
